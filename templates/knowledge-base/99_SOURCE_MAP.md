## On AI-generated sources

Where an AI summary was used as input, record it as such. Those documents reliably promote
contributions into ownership and attach team results to individuals. Where two of them
disagree, the disagreement usually marks exactly where one extrapolated — which makes it more
informative than either document's confident passages.
