# 10 — Claims register

Where a disputed or inflated claim is resolved **once, in writing**, so the same argument is
not had again in three months.

Open an entry whenever: two sources disagree, a claim sounds stronger than its evidence, a
number cannot be sourced, or the person decides something stays unpublished.

Give each a stable ID (`CR-01`, …) and reference it from wherever the claim appears.

## Notes on using this file

- **Downgrade, do not delete.** A claim moved from B to C is still usable, hedged. A deleted
  claim gets rediscovered and re-argued.
- **Record upgrades too.** The most valuable entries are often claims that were understated
  because nobody asked the follow-up question.
- **`do-not-publish` is a legitimate resolution.** Some facts are true, verifiable, and still
  the person's decision to keep off external documents.
