# 13 — Bullet library

Pre-written, externally safe CV bullets. Internal names already translated; every number
already carrying its correct attribution.

**Tag format:** `B-nn · Tier · Attribution · metric refs`

- Use a bullet **as written**, or tighten it. **Do not strengthen its verb.**
- **Never** convert a TEAM bullet to IND by dropping the qualifier.
- Aim for 5–7 bullets in an experience section; the rest are reserves for variants, cover
  letters and interviews.
