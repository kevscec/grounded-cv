## Deliberately not gaps

Honest absences in the profile itself, rather than holes in this knowledge base. These belong
in the capability map's absences section and are what make an honest fit assessment possible.

-

> **One caveat worth keeping.** People under-report experience outside their main job — side
> projects, volunteer infrastructure, unpaid technical roles. When an absence matters for a
> target role, ask once more in concrete terms before treating it as final.
