# 07 — Ways of working

Philosophy, patterns and methodology. Source material for cover letters, interview answers and
the "how do you work" question — rarely for CV bullets directly.

## 1. How this person decides what to build

The decision procedure, in their own words. Most valuable when it includes what they decide
*not* to do.

## 2. Recurring patterns

Things they do the same way every time, and why.

| Pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| | |

## 3. Methodologies practised

Named methods, and whether they are used as a working cycle or as a ceremony. Be honest about
which.

## 4. What happens after something ships

Monitoring, incident response, root-cause analysis, improvement. Usually the most
under-reported and most differentiating material a person has.
