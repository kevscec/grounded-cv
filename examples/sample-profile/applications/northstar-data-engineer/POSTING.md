## About the role

We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to own and extend the platform our analytics and
product teams depend on. You will work close to the people consuming the data, and you will be
on call for what you build.

- Manage warehouse cost and query performance as a first-class concern
- Partner with analysts and product managers to make data self-serve
- Participate in the on-call rotation

- 5+ years in data engineering

- Experience owning data quality and testing practice
- Comfortable with infrastructure as code
